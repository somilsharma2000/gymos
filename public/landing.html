Security & Trust

Your members' data. Protected like it's ours.

You're handing us your gym's most valuable asset — your member list. Here's exactly how we treat it.

Your data stays yours

Every member record, payment and lead is exportable anytime — no lock-in, no hostage data. Leave and you leave with everything.

Separated per gym

Each gym runs in its own account with its own members, staff and branches. Your data is never mixed with another gym's.

DPDP-aligned consent

Consent is captured at every lead form and member touchpoint, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act.

A signed agreement, in writing

Every gym gets a written data processing agreement before go-live — your legal protection under DPDP Sec 8(2), not a verbal promise.

Role-based access

Owners see money and reports. Desk staff see check-ins and leads. Nobody sees more than their role requires.

Payments over UPI rails

Members pay via UPI directly — we never hold or touch your money. Fees settle to your account, your ledger stays the source of truth.

Have a specific security question for your IT or legal team? Ask on the demo call — we'll answer in writing.
